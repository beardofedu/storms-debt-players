Table Safety

Tools and agreements to keep the table a good place to play

✅ The Short Version

This campaign explores dark themes — grief, coercion, divine power, moral choices under pressure. That's what makes it interesting. But the goal is always a table where everyone has a good time. The tools on this page exist to protect that goal, not to limit the story. Use them freely. No judgment, no drama.

Safety Tool #1

The X-Card

The X-Card is the simplest, most immediate safety tool at the table. It can be a physical card, a tapped table, or a typed "X" in your video chat — whatever the group agrees on.

How to Use It

🃏 In the moment

If content comes up in the game that you want to skip — for any reason — tap the X-Card or signal "X." No explanation needed. No questions asked. The DM adjusts and moves on.

🚫 No questions asked

You never have to explain why. "X" means "let's move past this." The table respects it immediately and without comment. This is not a disruption — it's the tool doing exactly what it's for.

Who Can Use It

Anyone. Players, the DM, an observer. Any time. Including retroactively — if something happened earlier and it's still sitting wrong, you can X-Card it after the fact and the DM can retcon or reframe.

The X-Card is not a critique of the DM or other players. It's a signal that says: "I need this adjusted." That's a healthy table. That's what we want.

Safety Tool #2

Lines & Veils

Lines and Veils are agreements the whole group makes before play — ideally at Session 0 — about what kinds of content will or won't appear in the campaign.

🚫 Lines

Content that will never appear in the game — full stop. Not off-screen, not implied, not referenced. Lines are hard limits that everyone agrees on as a group. If even one person draws a line, that content is out. No negotiating.

🌫️ Veils

Content that can exist in the world but happens off-screen — we "fade to black" rather than playing it out. The event can be real in the fiction without being depicted in play. Veils let the story acknowledge difficult things without dwelling on them.

📋 Session 0 Conversation

Lines & Veils are established as a group at Session 0. Every player contributes. They can also be updated during the campaign — if something comes up that needs to be added, bring it to the DM and it will be addressed. The list is never closed.

Safety Tool #3

Open Door Policy

Every player has the right to step away from the table at any time — during a scene, mid-session, whenever — without explanation. Take a break, step outside, skip a session. The table continues without judgment. Come back when you're ready.

If something in the campaign is consistently difficult for you and you'd rather address it before it becomes a problem, talk to the DM privately. That conversation is always available and always confidential.

What to Expect

Campaign Tone

The Storm's Debt is described as dark but hopeful. That means:

What "dark" means here
  • Real grief (Thortle's loss isn't swept under the rug)
  • Moral pressure — you'll face choices without clean answers
  • A powerful antagonist with real leverage over the party
  • Consequences that persist across sessions
  • Moments where things go badly and that matters
What "hopeful" means here
  • The party's bond is real and the story honors it
  • Warmth, humor, and joy are never out of place
  • Both the path of service and the path of defiance lead somewhere
  • Heroes' Hall is genuinely a home — a safe place in the story
  • The player characters matter. What they do matters.

🕯️ Grief as a Theme

Thortle's sacrifice is a real and present loss. The campaign won't rush past it, and it won't be used to torture you either. How your character grieves — or doesn't — is entirely yours to play. There's no right answer.

During Play

During and After Sessions

Checking In Mid-Session

The DM may occasionally pause to do a quick temperature check — especially after an intense scene. You can always signal that you need a pause. "Let's take five" is always a valid call from any player.

Debrief / Stars and Wishes

At the end of sessions (especially emotionally heavy ones), the table may do a brief debrief:

  • ⭐ Stars — something you appreciated about the session: a moment, a choice, another player's roleplay
  • 🌠 Wishes — something you'd like to see more of going forward (not complaints — requests for the future)

This is optional and low-pressure. It's a way to close the loop on big sessions and keep communication open.

💬 Questions or Concerns?

If something in the campaign — past, present, or anticipated — is affecting your enjoyment or wellbeing, reach out to the DM directly. These conversations are always confidential and always matter more than any scene in the game. The table works because the people at it are okay.
