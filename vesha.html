Level 13 Loxodon · Bard 4 / Cleric 9 (Twilight Domain)

Vesha

Share the dark, mend the wounded, and let no one lose heart while you're standing.

Open D&D Beyond
AC21
HP94
Speed30 ft.
Initiative+1
WIS20 (+5)
Save DC18
Channel Divinity3 / Long Rest

Default combat plan

  1. Before initiative: Vigilant Blessing to give one ally Advantage on their initiative roll.
  2. Turn 1: Cast Bless on up to three allies, or open with Twilight Sanctuary if the fight starts rough.
  3. Action: Channel Divinity — Divine Spark to heal an ally or blast an enemy at 30 ft.
  4. Melee moment: Moon Sickle for a quick hit while keeping Concentration.
  5. End of turn: Refresh Twilight Sanctuary's temp HP/charmed-frightened cleanse if allies are near.
⚔

On your turn

Attacks & Control

Channel Divinity — Divine SparkAction · 30 ft.

Choose a creature within 30 ft.: restore 2d8 + 5 HP, or force a DC 18 CON save — on a failure it takes 2d8 + 5 necrotic or radiant damage (half on a success).

Moon Sickle +2Attack · Simple, Light, Nick

+9 to hit · 1d4 + 4 damage — safe to swing without breaking Concentration on a leveled spell.

Sear UndeadChannel Divinity · vs. undead

Deal 1d8 radiant damage to an undead creature within range as part of your Channel Divinity uses.

Other options

Unarmed Strike: +7, 3 bludgeoning · Finger Guns: cantrip, +9/+10 to hit, 2d6 force at range.

🛡

Stay standing

Defensives

Twilight SanctuaryChannel Divinity · 30-ft. sphere

Create a 30-ft. dim-light sphere for 1 minute. On your turn, choose creatures inside to gain 1d6 + 9 temporary HP, or end one Charmed or Frightened condition on them.

Loxodon SerenityPassive

Advantage on saving throws against being Charmed or Frightened, plus a bonus on saving throws in general — Vesha resists mental control unusually well.

Numbers that matter

AC 21 · WIS and CHA saves proficient · Darkvision 300 ft. from Eyes of Night · high passive Perception/Insight.

✚

Temporary HP & recovery

Healing & Support

Divine Spark (heal side)Action · 30 ft.

Restore 2d8 + 5 HP to a creature within range — Vesha's fastest single-target heal, usable at range without Concentration.

Twilight Sanctuary (temp HP)Once per turn while active

Grant 1d6 + 9 temporary HP to a creature in the sphere each of your turns — stack this before an alpha strike lands on the party.

Eyes of Night1 / Long Rest

Share 300-ft. Darkvision with up to five allies for 1 hour — invaluable before a dungeon crawl or a night ambush.

Remember: as a Bard/Cleric, Vesha also has the full spell list to prepare from — check daily prepared spells for additional healing (Cure Wounds, Mass Healing Word, etc.) beyond Channel Divinity.
