The Party

Six adventurers bound by a storm god's debt

📜 Level 13 · Starting the Storm's Debt

All characters begin at Level 13. Returning characters carry everything they earned in Phandelver — their items, their scars, and the memory of Thortle.

🕯️ In Memory of Thortle

Thortle gave everything inside a purple worm to answer Talos's call and save Asteri. He was a member of the original party. His absence is a presence in every session that follows. How your character carries that grief — or doesn't — is yours to play.

Class Highlights at Level 13

At Level 13, your characters have access to powerful abilities that define high-tier play. A few key reminders:

Character Class(es) Notable Level 13 Features
Tyrael Paladin / Sorcerer Aura of Courage (Pal 10), 7th-level spell slots possible with multiclass; check your specific subclass features
Asteri Ranger 7th-level spells available (Ranger 13); check your subclass for capstone features around this tier
Jack Rogue / Warlock Uncanny Dodge + Evasion (Rogue); Mystic Arcanum for 7th-level spells (Warlock 13)
Marlo Druid 7th-level spells; Wild Shape improvements by subclass; Beast Spells if Moon Druid
Kaelen Barbarian (World Tree) Rage: Weapon of the Wilderness; check World Tree subclass features at 10/14 for your tier
Vesha Cleric / Bard 7th-level divine spells; Bardic Inspiration die increases at higher tiers; check your subclass
