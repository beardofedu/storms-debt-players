Level 13 Leonin · Paladin 7 / Sorcerer 6

Tyrael "Leo" Leonnister

Hit like a smite, heal like a lifeline, and let nothing near your allies survive it.

Open D&D Beyond
AC20
HP148
Speed35 ft.
Initiative+2
CHA20 (+5)
Save DC18
Lay on Hands35 HP Pool

Default combat plan

  1. Bonus Action: Twinned Metamagic on a buff spell, or move in with Wind Sprint.
  2. Action: Attack with the Longsword or Luminous War Pick, using Booming Blade if you expect a runner.
  3. Big hit needed: spend a Paladin spell slot on Divine Smite for extra radiant damage.
  4. Low on an ally? Lay on Hands (35 HP pool) instead of attacking.
  5. Support the line: Bless before the fight, then keep your Aura within 10–30 ft. of allies.
⚔

On your turn

Attacks & Control

Longsword +1Attack · Versatile

+10 to hit · 1d8 + 5 one-handed or 1d10 + 5 two-handed slashing.

Luminous War PickAttack · 1 charge

+10 to hit · 1d8 + 5 / 1d10 + 5 — its charge can add a radiant burst against a fiend or undead.

Divine SmiteOn a melee hit · Spend a spell slot

Add extra radiant damage on a successful melee hit; spend a higher-level slot for more damage, or expend one automatically against an undead or fiend target.

Booming BladeCantrip · replaces one attack

Melee hit, then +2d8 thunder damage if the target willingly moves before your next turn — great for pinning a target near your allies.

Other weapons

Moon-Touched Sword (Greatsword): +9, 2d6 + 4 · Spear of Talos: +9, 1d6 + 4/1d8 + 4 · Javelin: +10, 1d6 + 4, thrown 30/120.

🛡

Stay standing

Defensives

Divine HealthPassive

Immune to disease. Combined with heavy armor and a d10 hit die, Leo is built to stand in the front line and absorb hits.

Heavy Armor Master (feat)Passive

Reduce non-magical bludgeoning, piercing, and slashing damage by 3 while wearing heavy armor.

Aura of Protection / Aura of CouragePassive · 10–30 ft.

You and nearby allies add your CHA modifier (+5) to saving throws, and allies can't be Frightened while within your aura. Keep the party clustered near you.

Numbers that matter

AC 20 · WIS save +11, CHA save +15 · resistance to poison and psychic damage · Darkvision 60 ft.

✚

Temporary HP & recovery

Healing & Support

Lay on HandsAction · 35 HP pool

Touch a creature to restore HP from a shared 35-point pool (refills on a Long Rest), or spend 5 points to cure one disease or neutralize one poison instead.

Divine SenseBonus Action · Detect the unnatural

Sense the presence of celestials, fiends, and undead within 60 ft. — use it before entering an unfamiliar room.

Bless1st-level · Concentration

Up to three creatures add 1d4 to attack rolls and saving throws — cast it before the fight starts whenever possible.

Remember: Lay on Hands is a shared pool, not per-use HP — track how much you've spent across the whole adventuring day.

↯

Outside your turn

Reactions

Opportunity AttackReaction

Make one melee attack when a creature leaves your reach without Disengaging — a good moment to spend a smite if the hit lands.

Reaction check: Consider holding your reaction if an ally may need you to Twin a defensive spell instead.

Paladin + Sorcerer casting · +10 to hit, DC 18

Spells

Metamagic — Twinned SpellSorcery Points

Spend sorcery points to target a second creature with a spell that normally only affects one target — double up Bless-like buffs or single-target control on two targets at once.

Cantrips known

Booming Blade · Gust Barrier · Mage Hand · Message · Minor Illusion.

Known spells (partial)

Absorb Elements · Bless, plus the rest of your Paladin/Sorcerer spell list up to your available slot levels — check current slots before each fight.

Font of Magic

Convert leftover spell slots into sorcery points (or vice versa) between fights to fuel Metamagic or squeeze out one more smite.

🌀

Lock them down

Crowd Control

Booming BladeDeterrent

Punishes movement with 2d8 thunder — pin a dangerous target next to you instead of letting it reach the backline.

Aura of CouragePassive · 10–30 ft.

Denies enemies the Frightened condition as a control tool against your allies — stand centrally so the aura covers as much of the party as possible.

Twinned control spells

Use Twinned Spell on any single-target control spell you pick up (e.g., a hold or fear effect) to lock down two enemies with one slot.
