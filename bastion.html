Heroes' Hall

Your bastion in Phandalin — home base, safe haven, and yours to shape

The Bastion

Your Home in Phandalin

"The Alderman house, on the east rise. Restored, furnished, and yours. Every one of you. Phandalin takes care of its heroes."
— Sildar Hallwinter

Heroes' Hall is a large stone-and-timber house on the eastern edge of Phandalin, gifted to the party by the town council. It sits on a gentle rise overlooking the eastern orchards — thick stone walls on the ground floor, dark timber framing above, a slate roof, and a wide porch that wraps around the front.

Above the front door, a wooden plaque reads "Heroes' Hall" — carved by Pip Stonehill, the lettering slightly uneven but full of love.

🏠 What Is a Bastion?

A bastion is your personal stronghold — in this case, a shared house the whole party manages together. The bastion system (2024 DMG, Chapter 8) gives you facilities: special rooms that provide mechanical benefits between adventures. You collectively manage 12 facilities at the start of the campaign.

How It Works

The Bastion System

Facilities & Stewards

Heroes' Hall has 12 special facilities. Each character is the steward of 2 facilities — they manage it and issue its orders each Bastion Turn. Any character can benefit from any facility; stewards just run the place.

Bastion Turns

Every 7 in-game days, the DM calls for a Bastion Turn. Each steward issues one order to each of their facilities. Your facilities work on those orders while you're adventuring — crafting items, gathering intel, earning gold.

Order Type What It Does
CraftCreate an item — potion, scroll, weapon, etc.
ResearchLearn information about a topic, creature, or magic item
TradeBuy or sell goods, earn income
RecruitHire hirelings or attract allies to your cause
HarvestGather herbs, spell components, or raw materials
EmpowerGain a temporary magical benefit for the party

Not every facility supports every order — each has specific orders it can handle.

What Each Facility Does

Facility Guide

Basic Facilities (Level 5+)

🛏️ Bedroom
Everyone gets one free — doesn't count against the 12 slots
Long rest benefits, personal storage. Make it yours — decorate it however you like.
🐴 Stable
Steward: Asteri · Backup: Kaelen
Houses mounts and animal companions. Rocket lives here. Issue orders to care for, train, or use your animals.
📦 Storehouse
Steward: Kaelen · Backup: Anyone
Stores goods, manages party supplies, earns income through trade orders. Your logistical hub.

Level 9 Facilities

🔮 Arcane Study
Steward: Marlo · Backup: Vesha
Craft spell scrolls, research arcane topics. Issue a Craft order to produce a scroll, or Research to dig into magical lore.
🌿 Garden
Steward: Asteri · Backup: Marlo
Grow herbs and harvest potion ingredients. Issue Harvest orders to stock up on spell components and alchemical materials.
📚 Library
Steward: Marlo · Backup: Tyrael
Research lore, identify items, gain proficiency benefits. Issue Research orders to learn about creatures, locations, or mysteries.
⛪ Sanctuary
Steward: Vesha · Backup: Tyrael
Healing services, curing conditions, divine communion. Between adventures, issue orders to restore the party or seek divine insight.
⚒️ Smithy
Steward: Tyrael · Backup: Kaelen
Craft and enhance weapons and armor. Issue Craft orders to make or upgrade equipment between sessions.
🏋️ Training Area
Steward: Kaelen · Backup: Tyrael
Gain temporary combat benefits, train hirelings. Issue Empower orders to prepare for upcoming fights.
🏆 Trophy Room
Steward: Jack · Backup: Anyone
Display trophies from defeated foes to gain Heroic Inspiration. The more impressive the collection, the better the benefit.

Level 13 Facilities

🍺 Pub
Steward: Jack · Backup: Vesha
Gather rumors, recruit contacts, earn gold. Jack's speakeasy in the wine cellar. Issue Recruit or Trade orders to work the crowd.
⚔️ War Room
Steward: Tyrael · Backup: Kaelen
Plan missions, gain tactical advantages on upcoming encounters. Issue Research or Empower orders to prepare the party strategically.
🔭 Observatory
Steward: Vesha · Backup: Marlo
Track celestial events, gain divination benefits. Issue Research or Empower orders to read the stars for advantages ahead.

🏡 Make It Yours

Decorate your room, name your spaces, build a Thortle memorial in the garden, install the speakeasy. Heroes' Hall is yours — treat it like home.

Caretaker

Pip Stonehill

When the party is away adventuring, Pip Stonehill — the innkeepers' son the party helped rescue — has self-appointed himself as Heroes' Hall's caretaker. He keeps the fire lit, waters the garden, and leaves notes about town gossip on the kitchen table.

He carved the sign above the door. His letters are a little uneven. He's very proud of it.
