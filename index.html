The Campaign

What Is The Storm's Debt?

The Storm's Debt is a Levels 13–20 campaign continuing directly from the events of Phandelver. You've defeated the mind flayer threat beneath Phandalin. The town is safe. But Thortle is gone — and his sacrifice didn't come free.

When Thortle called upon Talos, the Stormlord for Divine Intervention, Talos answered. The worm exploded. The party survived. Thortle vanished. And now the god who answered that prayer has come to collect what's owed — from you.

Do you serve the Stormlord — or do you defy a god?

Both paths are real. Both lead somewhere. Your choices drive the story.

Quick Facts

Campaign at a Glance

🎯 Starting Level
Level 13

Continuing characters keep all items from Phandelver. New characters begin at Level 13 with equipment provided.

🗺️ Setting
Sword Coast

Centered on Phandalin and the surrounding Sword Coast region. The world is post-Phandelver — consequences are real.

🏠 Home Base
Heroes' Hall

A house in Phandalin gifted to the party by the town council. See the Bastion page for details.

🎭 Tone
Dark but hopeful

High stakes, real grief, real consequences — and room for warmth, loyalty, and moments of levity.

📖 Acts
Four acts

The campaign runs four acts across Levels 13–20. The story responds to player choices at every major junction.

🛡️ Safety
Tools at the table

We use Lines & Veils, the X-Card, and an open door policy. See the Safety page.

Preparation

What to Do Before Session 1

Returning Players

  • Review your Level 13 character sheet — spells, abilities, items
  • Think about how your character is feeling in the aftermath of Thortle's death
  • Come with your character's emotional state, not a plan
  • No spoilers needed — let the story unfold at the table

New Players (Kaelen / Vesha)

  • Review your character's backstory — your DM will send it separately
  • Your character knows something unusual happened in Phandalin
  • You don't need to know the party's full history — your character doesn't either
  • Come with questions, not answers
