Level 13 Harengon · Rogue 3 / Warlock 10

"Flopsy" Jack

Get the advantage, land the sneaky hit, and be somewhere else before it matters.

Open D&D Beyond
AC19
HP107
Speed40 ft.
Initiative+14
DEX20 (+5)
Save DC17
Pact Slots2 (5th) / Short Rest

Default combat plan

  1. Bonus Action: Cunning Action to Hide or reposition for Advantage before you swing.
  2. Action: Attack twice with Gambler's Blade (Pact of the Blade + Thirsting Blade).
  3. First hit with Advantage: add Sneak Attack (2d6).
  4. Need range or an opener? Eldritch Blast for 3 beams instead of melee.
  5. Need control? Whip (10-ft. reach, Slow) or Booming Blade to punish a runner.
⚔

On your turn

Attacks & Control

Gambler's Blade (Rapier)Attack · Pact Weapon

+13 to hit · finesse · 1d8 + 10 slashing.

Vex mastery: on a hit, gain Advantage on your next attack roll against that same target before the end of your next turn.

Sneak AttackOnce per turn · 2d6

Add 2d6 damage to one hit each turn when you have Advantage on the attack, or when another enemy of the target is within 5 ft. of it and you don't have Disadvantage.

Eldritch BlastCantrip · 120 ft.

+9 to hit, three beams of 1d10 + 4 force each (Agonizing Blast), can target the same or different creatures.

Smart-weapon cantripsReplaces one weapon attack

Booming Blade: melee hit, then +2d8 thunder if the target willingly moves before your next turn.

Green-Flame Blade: melee hit, then 2d8 fire to another creature within 5 ft. of the target.

Other weapons

Whip: +10, 10-ft. reach, 1d4 + 7, Slow mastery · Shortbow: +10, 1d6 + 5, Vex · Crossbow, Heavy: +10, 1d10 + 5.

Stay standing

Defensives

Cunning ActionBonus Action

Dash, Disengage, or Hide for free every turn. Use it to break line of sight, escape a grapple threat, or set up Advantage before your Action.

Accursed SpecterHexblade patron feature

When you or an ally reduces a humanoid to 0 HP, you can bind its spirit as a temporary Accursed Specter that fights alongside you and can soak attacks meant for the party.

Numbers that matter

AC 19 · strong DEX/CON/CHA saves · Darkvision 120 ft. · high passive Stealth from DEX 20 and rogue training.

✚

Temporary HP & recovery

Healing & Support

Party utilitySkills & face

Jack's job is information and options, not restoring HP: leverage Expertise skills and high CHA to talk, scout, and disarm problems before they become damage.

Remember: Jack has no direct healing spell on the sheet. Keep him alive with distance, Cunning Action, and Advantage-fishing instead of standing in the open.

Outside your turn

Reactions

Opportunity AttackReaction

Make one melee attack when a creature you can see leaves your reach without Disengaging. With the Whip out, this threatens 10 ft.

Reaction check: Before a creature leaves your reach, decide whether the Opportunity Attack or holding the reaction for a better opening serves you more.
