Level 13 Kobold · Druid

Detective Marlo Kud

Shape into something with teeth, whip the battlefield into shape, and read every clue the terrain gives you.

Open D&D Beyond
AC18
HP107
Speed30 ft.
Initiative+1
WIS20 (+5)
Save DC18
Draconic Cry5 / Long Rest

Default combat plan

  1. Bonus Action: Draconic Cry to give allies Advantage before the fight opens.
  2. Action: Wild Shape into a combat form (Deep Rothé, Elk, or Hyena) or cast a spell as yourself.
  3. In beast form: use the form's signature attack — Charge/Gore, Hooves/Ram, or Bite.
  4. At range: Staff of Swarming Insects or Thorn Whip to control and chip damage.
  5. Battlefield control: Nature's Shield or Frostbite to blunt a dangerous attacker.
⚔

On your turn

Attacks & Control

Wild Shape — Deep RothéCharge / Gore

Powerful melee beast form. Gore and Charge attacks hit hard, and Charge can knock a target prone on a failed STR save if you moved at least 10 ft. beforehand.

Wild Shape — ElkHooves / Ram

Faster, mobile beast form. Ram can push a struck creature back and knock it prone if it fails a STR save after you charge it.

Wild Shape — HyenaBite

Pack-tactics form — best when an ally is adjacent to the same target, granting Advantage on the Bite attack.

Staff of Swarming Insects+9 to hit · 5/10 charges

1d8 + 4 damage, and it can create a swarm of insects that deters enemies from an area.

As yourself

Scimitar: +10 to hit, 1d6 + 4 · Thorn Whip: cantrip, pulls the target 10 ft. closer to you on a hit.

🛡

Stay standing

Defensives

Wild ShapeAction

Turn into a beast form with its own HP pool — damage that would drop Marlo instead absorbs into the form's HP first, protecting his real HP total.

Nature's ShieldCantrip · Reaction-timed buff

Impose Disadvantage on an incoming attack against you or an ally, turning a likely hit into a likely miss.

Kobold Legacy — Draconic CryBonus Action · 5 / Long Rest

Give allies within range Advantage on their attack rolls, stacking action economy in the party's favor before the real damage starts.

Numbers that matter

AC 18 · INT and WIS saves both +10 · Passive Perception/Investigation 25 (Observant feat) · Resilient (CON) shores up Concentration saves.

✚

Temporary HP & recovery

Healing & Support

Full Druid spell listPrepared caster

As a full Druid, Marlo can prepare healing spells (like Cure Wounds or Mass Cure Wounds) each day when the party needs recovery instead of Wild Shape utility — check prepared spells before each rest.

GuidanceCantrip

+1d4 to an ally's ability check — use it before a risky skill check or saving throw where the group needs certainty.

Remember: Wild Shape locks out spellcasting while transformed. Decide before you shift whether you'll need a spell this fight.

Outside your turn

Reactions

Opportunity AttackReaction

Make one attack — beast form attack while Wild Shaped, or Scimitar/Thorn Whip otherwise — when a creature leaves your reach without Disengaging.

Reaction check: Nature's Shield is best held for the attack most likely to actually connect with you or a squishy ally.

🌀

Lock them down

Crowd Control

FrostbiteCantrip · CON save

On a failed save, the target has Disadvantage on its next attack roll before your next turn — great debuff before a squishy ally's turn.

Thorn WhipCantrip · Hit effect

Pulls the target up to 10 ft. toward you — drag enemies out of a protected position or away from the party's backline.

Beast form prone effectsDeep Rothé / Elk charge

After moving 10+ ft. before the attack, a failed STR save knocks the target prone, denying it actions and giving melee allies Advantage.

Nature's Shield

Disadvantage on an incoming attack is control aimed at the enemy's action economy, not just a defensive buff.

Shape choiceDeep Rothé/Elk for prone control, Hyena when an ally is already engaged with the target.
