Level 13 Aarakocra · Ranger

Asteri

Mark the target, drop it from above, and never let it walk away.

Open D&D Beyond
AC17
HP108
Speed35 ft. (Fly)
Initiative+5
DEX20 (+5)
Save DC17
Tireless1d8 + 4 Temp HP

Default combat plan

  1. Bonus Action: Cast Hunter's Mark on the priority target if it isn't already active.
  2. Action: Attack twice with the Bow of the Verdant Hunt from range.
  3. Melee threat closing in? Use flight to reposition to high ground or out of reach.
  4. Marked target trying to flee? Ensnaring Strike or Beast of the Land's prone effect to stop it.
  5. Before a priority fight: activate Hunter's Focus, then expend a spell slot to infuse an arrow with Nature's Arrow.
  6. After a rest: use Bolstering Performance to give the party temporary HP; Tireless is another option for one creature.
⚔

On your turn

Attacks & Control

Bow of the Verdant HuntAttack · 2 per action

+14 to hit · 150/600 ft. range · 1d8 + 9 damage per shot.

Sharpshooter — Verdant HuntTrade accuracy for damage

+9 to hit · 1d8 + 19 damage — take the −5/+10 trade when the target is easy to hit or already has Advantage against it.

Hunter's Focus1 / Long Rest · 1 minute

Focus on one target for 1 minute. Each successful attack you make against it with this bow increases your damage against that target by 1d6.

Unyielding Precision1 / Short Rest

When you miss a creature with an attack using this bow, reroll the attack once. You must use the second roll, even if it is lower.

Nature's Arrow1 / Short Rest · Spell slot

Expend a spell slot of 1st level or higher to infuse an arrow. On a hit, it deals an extra 2d6 force or poison damage (your choice), and that damage bypasses resistance to its type.

Vinebound StrikeOn a bow hit · DC 15 STR

Choose to ensnare the target in vines. On a failed Strength save, it is Restrained until the start of your next turn and takes 1d6 poison damage at the start of each of its turns while restrained.

Hunter's MarkBonus Action · Concentration

+1d6 damage on every hit against the marked target, and you can sense its direction — keep this running on the biggest threat all fight.

Quiver of the Lionshield60 arrows · 1 / Long Rest

Holds up to 60 arrows and keeps them dry and organized. Once per Long Rest, draw and fire an arrow as a free action, with no action cost.

Beast of the Land Attack+4 to hit

1d8 + 5 / 1d10 + 5 — on a hit after the beast moves, the target makes a STR save or is knocked prone.

Other options

Talons: +6, 1d6 + 1 · Unarmed Strike: +6, 2 bludgeoning.

🛡

Stay standing

Defensives

FlightAarakocra racial trait

A flying speed lets Asteri stay out of melee reach entirely — use verticality and range to avoid ever being adjacent to a melee threat.

Deft Explorer — TirelessAction · Limited uses / Long Rest

Grant yourself or an ally 1d8 + 4 temporary HP, and reduce one level of Exhaustion — good insurance before a big fight or after a long trek.

Numbers that matter

AC 17 · high DEX-based saves and passive Perception 19 · light/medium armor and shield proficiency.

✚

Temporary HP & recovery

Healing & Support

Bolstering PerformanceAfter each Short or Long Rest

Give an inspiring speech, song, or dance, choosing up to six allies within 30 ft. (including yourself) who witness it. Each gains temporary HP equal to 13 + the modifier of the ability increased by this feat.

TirelessDeft Explorer

Asteri's main form of "healing" is temporary HP via Tireless, not direct restoration — use it proactively before damage lands, not reactively after.

Ranger spell supportPrepared spells

Check prepared Ranger spells for support options (like Cure Wounds, if taken) beyond the damage/control spells listed here.

Remember: Asteri is primarily a damage and control character — lean on Leo or Vesha for sustained party healing, and use Tireless to cushion the hit before it happens.

Outside your turn

Reactions

Opportunity AttackReaction

Make one attack when a creature leaves your reach without Disengaging — with flight, this rarely comes up, since Asteri is usually already out of reach.

Reaction check: Prefer flying away over standing still to bait an Opportunity Attack — Asteri's damage comes from the bow, not melee.

Ranger casting · +9 to hit, DC 17

Spells

The backbone of Asteri's damage output — +1d6 per hit on the marked target. Recast whenever it drops or the target changes.

Ensnaring Strike1st-level · On a weapon hit

The struck target must succeed on a STR save or become Restrained by grasping vines until it breaks free — a strong lockdown option against a single dangerous melee attacker.

🌀

Lock them down

Crowd Control

Ensnaring StrikeDC 17 STR save

Restrains the target on a failed save, and it takes damage again each turn it tries and fails to escape — ideal on a melee brute you need to remove from the fight.

Beast of the Land — AttackSTR save

A failed save after the beast moves knocks the target prone, giving melee allies Advantage and slowing the target's next actions.

Hunter's Mark tracking

Sensing the marked creature's direction denies it the ability to simply vanish from the fight — control through information as much as damage.
