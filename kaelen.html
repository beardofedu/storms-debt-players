Level 13 Firbolg · Path of the World Tree

Kaelen Rootwalker

Stand tall. Reach far. Pull friends to safety and enemies into the roots.

Open D&D Beyond
AC18
HP148
Speed40 ft.
Initiative+2
STR20 (+5)
Save DC18
Rages5 / Long Rest

Default combat plan

  1. Bonus Action: Rage, then move up to 20 ft. with Instinctive Pounce.
  2. Action: Attack twice with Rootcleaver from up to 15 ft. away.
  3. First good hit: add Heavy Weapon Master and Predatory Growth.
  4. Choose control: Cleave, Push, Topple, or trade Reckless Advantage for a Brutal Strike.
  5. Start each turn: give a nearby ally temporary HP with Life-Giving Force.
⚔

On your turn

Attacks & Control

RootcleaverAttack · 2 per action

+12 to hit · 15-ft. reach · 1d12 + 10 slashing while Raging (1d12 + 7 otherwise).

It is a +2 magic greataxe and returns to your hand at the end of your turn if it is within 30 ft. and not held by another creature.

Stack damage on one hitOnce per turn

Heavy Weapon Master: +5 damage on a Heavy-weapon hit made as part of the Attack action.

Predatory Growth: while Raging, +1d6 necrotic damage and gain that much temporary HP.

Weapon masteryOn a hit

Cleave: once per turn, attack a second creature within 5 ft. of the first. Do not add STR to the second hit's damage.

Battering Roots: on your turn, Rootcleaver has 15-ft. reach and can also Push 10 ft. or Topple (DC 18 CON) in addition to Cleave.

Brutal StrikeTrade Reckless Advantage

Give up Advantage from Reckless Attack on one attack. On a hit, deal +1d10 weapon damage and choose one:

Forceful: push 15 ft.; follow half speed safely. Hamstring: speed −15 ft. until your next turn. Staggering: Disadvantage on next save; no opportunity attacks. Sundering: next ally attack gets +5.
Grasp of the Old RootsNo action · At will

After striking the ground or a creature, chosen creatures in 15 ft. make a DC 18 STR save. Fail: Restrained through the end of your next turn. Success: speed halved until the start of your next turn. The area is difficult terrain through the end of your next turn.

Other attacks

Thorn Whip: +7, 30 ft., 3d6 piercing and pull 10 ft. · Unarmed: +10, 9 bludgeoning while Raging; Grapple or Shove DC 18.

🛡

Stay standing

Defensives

RageBonus Action · 5 / Long Rest

Resistance to bludgeoning, piercing, and slashing; Advantage on STR checks and STR saves; +3 damage with STR-based attacks.

Regain one use on a Short Rest. Extend it with a Bonus Action when needed; maximum duration 10 minutes.

Relentless RageWhen dropped to 0 HP

While Raging, make a DC 10 CON save. On success, stay up at 26 HP. DC rises by 5 after each success and resets on a Short or Long Rest.

Hidden StepBonus Action · 5 / Long Rest

Become Invisible until the start of your next turn, or until you attack, deal damage, or force a saving throw.

Gear defenses

Ring + Cloak of Protection: their AC and saving throw bonuses are already included. Adamantine chain shirt: critical hits against you become normal hits.

✚

Temporary HP & recovery

Healing & Support

Vitality of the TreeWhen Rage begins

Gain 13 temporary HP. Temporary HP does not stack; keep whichever amount is higher.

Life-Giving ForceStart of every Raging turn

Choose another creature within 10 ft. to gain 3d6 temporary HP. Any remaining points vanish when your Rage ends.

Predatory GrowthOnce per turn while Raging

When Rootcleaver hits, its extra 1d6 necrotic damage also gives you the same amount of temporary HP.

Remember: Kaelen does not have direct healing on the sheet. These abilities prevent damage or keep him standing; they do not restore another creature's lost HP.

↯

Outside your turn

Reactions

Branches of the TreeReaction · While Raging

When a creature you can see starts its turn within 30 ft., teleport it to an empty space within 5 ft. of you. An unwilling target makes a DC 18 STR save; on a failed save, you can also reduce its speed to 0 for that turn.

Use it to rescue an ally, pull an enemy away from a vulnerable friend, or lock a target beside you.

Opportunity AttackReaction

Make one melee attack when a creature leaves your reach without Disengaging.

Important: Battering Roots extends reach only on your turn, so your normal opportunity-attack reach is 5 ft.

Reaction check: At the start of every nearby creature's turn, ask whether Branches of the Tree would improve the battlefield.

Reckless AttackAdvantage for you, then attacks against you have Advantage until your next turn.
HewAfter a melee critical hit or dropping a creature to 0 HP, attack again with that weapon as a Bonus Action.
Primal KnowledgeWhile Raging, use STR for Acrobatics, Intimidation, Perception, Stealth, or Survival.
