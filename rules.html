Core Mechanics

Concentration

Many powerful spells require Concentration. Key rules at high levels:

  • You can only concentrate on one spell at a time. Casting a second concentration spell ends the first.
  • When you take damage while concentrating, make a Constitution saving throw: DC = 10 or half the damage taken, whichever is higher.
  • Failing the save breaks concentration immediately.
  • War Caster (feat) gives advantage on these saves and lets you cast concentration spells as opportunity attacks.
  • Resilient (Constitution) gives proficiency on Constitution saves, including concentration.

💡 Protecting Concentration

At high levels, many of your best spells (Forcecage, Dominate Monster, Power Word Stun) are concentration. Positioning yourself out of harm's way and having party members protect you is a real tactical consideration.

Death Saving Throws

When you drop to 0 HP you are unconscious and must make death saves on your turn:

  • Roll a d20 (no modifier). 10+ = success. 9 or below = failure.
  • 3 successes = stable (no longer dying, but still unconscious at 0 HP).
  • 3 failures = dead.
  • Rolling a 1 counts as 2 failures.
  • Rolling a 20 = regain 1 HP and regain consciousness immediately.
  • Taking any damage while at 0 HP = 1 automatic failure (2 if from a critical hit).
  • Any healing (even 1 HP) stabilizes you and brings you conscious.
  • The Spare the Dying cantrip immediately stabilizes without spending HP.

⚠️ High-Level Enemies Hit Hard

At Levels 13+, enemies deal massive damage. A single hit can take you from full HP to 0 — and the excess damage can kill you outright if it equals or exceeds your maximum HP.

Action Economy

Every character gets the following on their turn:

Action TypeWhat It Includes
Action Attack, Cast a Spell, Dash, Disengage, Dodge, Help, Hide, Ready, Search, Use Object, Improvise
Bonus Action Class features (Cunning Action, Wild Shape, etc.), two-weapon fighting, some spells — only if a feature grants it
Reaction Opportunity Attack, Shield spell, Counterspell, Hellish Rebuke — once per round, triggered by a specific event
Movement Up to your speed, split before/after action; free climbing/swimming (half speed unless you have a climb/swim speed)
Free Actions Talking, dropping an item, brief communication — DM discretion for anything significant

💡 Bonus Actions Are Limited

You can only use a Bonus Action if something specifically grants you one. You can't "save" unused bonus actions. One per turn.

Short Rest & Long Rest

⏱️ Short Rest
~1 hour of light activity
  • Spend Hit Dice to regain HP (add your Constitution modifier to each die)
  • Warlock: recover all Pact Magic spell slots
  • Some class features recharge on a Short Rest
  • You can take up to 2 Short Rests per Long Rest
🌙 Long Rest
~8 hours, mostly sleep
  • Regain all HP
  • Regain all spent spell slots
  • Regain up to half your maximum Hit Dice
  • Most class features recharge on a Long Rest
  • Must have at least 1 HP to begin; only 1 Long Rest per 24 hours

Legendary Creatures

At Level 13, you'll regularly face creatures with Legendary Actions and possibly Lair Actions.

⭐ Legendary Actions

Taken at the end of another creature's turn. Most legendary monsters have 3 per round (reset at the start of their turn). Common options include extra attacks, special abilities, or repositioning. Watch for them — they often happen right after your turn.

🏔️ Lair Actions

Triggered on Initiative count 20 (losing ties), if the creature is in its lair. These are environmental effects — tremors, magical hazards, summoned creatures. The DM will announce when a creature has Lair Actions.

⚡ High Level = High Stakes

At this tier, some enemies also have Resistances, Immunities, and Saving Throw bonuses that make status effects unreliable. Always have a backup plan when your save-or-suck spell fizzles.
